Unslump

Gentle reminders to stand up, fix your posture and rest your eyes, plus plank and stretch timers for your desk day.

Listed in the stores as "Unslump: Stand & Stretch". For iPhone and Android.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Three reminder types: stand up, posture check and 20-20-20 eye rest, each with its own interval.
  • Active hours and weekdays, so reminders stop after work and at weekends (custom schedules are Pro).
  • Answer from the notification: "I stood up", "Fixed it" or "Pause 1 h" without opening the app.
  • Plank timer with your best hold, a 20-second eye-rest timer and a guided desk-stretch routine (Pro).
  • Today's counts, your streak and weekly bars of what you actually did.
  • Pause for an hour or until tomorrow in one tap. Ten languages.

Free and Pro

Free includes one reminder of your choice at the interval you pick, snooze and pause, the plank timer, the eye-rest timer, 25 + 5-minute focus blocks, today's counts, your streak, the widget and export and restore. Pro adds all three reminders at once, your own hours and weekdays for each, five stretch routines in short and full lengths, your own focus lengths, automatic stand detection from Apple Health, and full history with weekly stats. Pro is a monthly or annual subscription (the annual plan starts with a 7-day free trial), or a one-time lifetime purchase, billed by the App Store or Google Play.
