Splashlab

Test in, doses out. Enter your readings and get exact amounts of chlorine, acid and more, in cups and pounds.

Listed in the stores as "Splashlab: Pool Calculator". For iPhone and Android.

What it does

  • Enter free chlorine, combined chlorine, pH, alkalinity, calcium, CYA and salt from any test kit or strip.
  • Get a step-by-step dosing plan in the units on the product label, ordered so each step does not undo the last.
  • Targets follow the TroubleFreePool (TFP) method, including the free-chlorine to CYA relationship and a salt-water generator mode (Pro).
  • A pool profile with a volume helper for rectangle, round, oval and kidney shapes.
  • History with free-chlorine and pH trend charts and a shaded target band, plus test reminders (Pro).
  • A clear reminder on every result: calculations are estimates, so always follow the product label.
