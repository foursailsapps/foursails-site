Symptom Tracker

Log a symptom in ten seconds, see what tends to set it off, and walk into your next appointment with a one-page summary.

Listed in the stores as "Symptom Tracker: Daily Log". For iPhone and Android.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Log any symptom in your own words, rate it 1 to 10 and tap possible triggers. Your recent symptoms become one-tap shortcuts.
  • A medications log with the last dose filled in for you.
  • Charts of each symptom over 7, 30 or 90 days.
  • A plain comparison of how bad a symptom tends to be with and without each trigger (Pro).
  • A one-page summary and CSV files to share with your doctor (Pro).
  • An optional daily check-in reminder, off by default. Ten languages.

Free and Pro

Free includes unlimited logging, suggestions, the medications log, 7- and 30-day charts, the daily check-in and the last 30 days of history. Pro adds your full history, the 90-day range, the with-and-without trigger comparisons, and the doctor summary and CSV exports. Pro is a weekly subscription with a free trial, or an annual subscription, billed by the App Store or Google Play.
