Newnest

A moving timeline built from your move date, the utilities and address changes people forget, and a box inventory you can search.

Listed in the stores as "Newnest: Moving Checklist". For iPhone and Android.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

Coming soon. Newnest is in final testing and store links will appear here when it is live.

What it does

  • Set your move date and get 23 dated tasks, from eight weeks before to a month after.
  • Utilities to set up or cancel and the people and places that need your new address.
  • Your state's typical deadline for updating your driver's licence, used to date that task (US).
  • Numbered boxes by room with contents, fragile tags and a packed tick; search finds the kettle.
  • Reminders on each task's due day and as moving day gets close, plus a countdown widget.
  • Add your own tasks; ten languages.

Free and Pro

Free includes the full timeline, the utilities and address lists, reminders, the widget, the moving budget, box photos, up to 10 tasks of your own and 20 boxes. Pro adds unlimited tasks and boxes, printable QR box labels, a box list PDF, a CSV of your boxes and a JSON export of everything. Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription), billed by the App Store or Google Play.
