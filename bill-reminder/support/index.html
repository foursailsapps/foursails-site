Paydue support

Answers to common questions about Paydue for iPhone and Android.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app connect to my bank?
No, and it never will. You enter bills yourself, which means there is nothing to link and no financial login that could leak.
What happens when I mark a bill paid?
The app records a payment and moves the bill to its next due date. If you pay late, it still only moves forward one period, so a skipped month shows as overdue. Tap Undo, or delete the payment from the bill's history, to roll it back.
How do I add the widget?
On iPhone, touch and hold the home screen, tap the + button and search for Paydue. On Android, touch and hold the home screen, choose Widgets and find Paydue, or use "Add widget" in the app's Settings.
How does monthly billing handle the 31st?
A bill due on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months (for example 28 February) and goes back to the 31st when the month allows it.
What is free and what is Pro?
Free includes up to 5 bills with reminders (with Mark paid from the notification), categories, monthly totals, the calendar, the widget, App lock and backup and restore. Pro adds unlimited bills, your full payment history with spending charts, the payday forecast and CSV export. Pro is a monthly or annual subscription (the annual plan starts with a 7-day free trial), or a one-time lifetime purchase, billed by the App Store or Google Play.
I bought Pro but it is locked on my new phone. How do I restore it?
Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it, then go to Settings, open the Pro screen and tap "Restore purchases". Subscriptions are managed in your App Store or Google Play account settings, not inside the app.
Is my data backed up? Can I move it to a new phone?
Your data is stored only on your phone (see the privacy policy). Your phone's own iCloud or Google backup may include it. Before switching phones, export your data from Settings and keep the file somewhere safe; we are working on import for a future version.
