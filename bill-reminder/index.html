Paydue

Every bill, every due date, a reminder before it is due. No bank linking, nothing to connect.

Listed in the stores as "Paydue: Bill Reminder & Alerts". For iPhone and Android.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Add a bill in seconds: name, amount, due date and how often it repeats (weekly, monthly, yearly or custom).
  • Reminders a few days before and on the due day, at the time you choose.
  • Mark a bill paid and the next due date is set for you, with Undo if you tap by mistake.
  • Autopay bills settle themselves once their due date passes.
  • A month calendar with due, paid and overdue markers, and a home-screen widget showing what is next.
  • Categories, monthly totals, payment history and CSV export (Pro). Ten languages.

Free and Pro

Free includes up to 5 bills, reminders (with Mark paid from the notification), the widget, App lock and JSON backup. Pro adds unlimited bills, categories, month totals, payment history and CSV export. Pro is a monthly or annual subscription (the annual plan starts with a 7-day free trial), or a one-time lifetime purchase, billed by the App Store or Google Play.
