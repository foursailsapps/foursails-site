Privacy at Four Sails

Effective 16 October 2026.

This website

foursailsapps.com collects nothing. There are no analytics, no cookies, no sign-up forms, no embedded third-party content and no fonts loaded from outside servers. The pages are static files.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record visitor IP addresses in server logs for security and to operate the service; see the GitHub Pages data collection note. We do not have access to any per-visitor information.

If you email us, we keep your message so we can reply and follow up. We do not add you to any list.

Our apps

Each app has its own policy describing exactly what it stores. The common thread: data stays on your device, there are no accounts, no analytics and no ads, in-app purchases are handled by RevenueCat, and an app that crashes sends an anonymous crash report to Sentry.

Two apps built for couples, Heartcount and I Do, can optionally share with a partner's phone. Only if you turn that on does the app sign in anonymously to a sync server (run for us by Supabase) and keep a copy of what you share there; their policies explain exactly what and how to remove it. Flareday and Bumpday keep health information on your phone only.
