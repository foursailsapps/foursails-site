I Do

Days to go on your home screen, a checklist that knows your date, and your vendors in one place. Shared with your partner.

Listed in the stores as "I Do: Wedding Countdown & Plan". For iPhone and Android.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

Coming soon. I Do is in final testing and store links will appear here when it is live.

What it does

  • A big "days to go" countdown, then "married N days" once the day has passed.
  • A starter checklist of 24 common tasks, each dated from your wedding day; add your own.
  • Vendors with category, contact, cost and booked status, with a running total.
  • Reminders when tasks are due and as the day gets close (100, 30, 7 and 1 days before).
  • Optional sharing with your partner's phone: scan a QR code to pair. What you share is end-to-end encrypted.
  • A home-screen widget with the countdown and the next tasks. Ten languages.

Free and Pro

Free includes the countdown with your photo, reminders, the budget, joining your partner's shared plan, up to 15 tasks, 3 vendors and 20 guests. Pro adds inviting your partner to plan together, the home-screen widget, unlimited tasks, vendors and guests, and JSON export. Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription), billed by the App Store or Google Play.

Privacy and support

I Do keeps your data on your device and has no ads, accounts or analytics. Sharing with a partner is optional and only starts when you pair the two phones. Read the I Do privacy policy or visit I Do support for answers to common questions. Email wedding-countdown@foursailsapps.com any time.
