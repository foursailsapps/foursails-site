Calmwater privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026. Applies to the Calmwater app for iOS and Android, published by Four Sails.

The short version: Calmwater keeps everything you enter on your own device. We do not run accounts, we do not collect analytics, we show no ads, and we never sell data. The only other third parties are the company that handles in-app purchases and the crash-reporting service, which hears from the app only when something goes wrong.

What the app stores, and where

Calmwater saves the information you enter in a local database (SQLite) and the app's settings storage, both inside the app's private storage on your phone. That includes:

  • Sessions you complete: date, start time, planned and actual length, and the bell settings used.
  • If you add them after a sit, a mood (on a five-step scale from restless to very calm) and a short note.
  • Your named presets and settings, such as bell choices, the daily reminder time and your bells through the day.

None of this leaves your device unless you export it yourself. We have no server that receives it, and we cannot see it.

Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include the app's data, under Apple's or Google's terms, if you have backups turned on.

Apple Health and Health Connect (optional)

If you turn on saving sessions in Settings (Apple Health on iPhone, Health Connect on Android), Calmwater asks for permission to write, and when a session ends saves its start and end time on your phone: on iPhone to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes, on Android to Health Connect as a meditation (mindfulness) session. That is all it writes; your mood and note are never written, and it never reads anything from Apple Health or Health Connect. The data is handled by Apple Health or Health Connect on your phone under Apple's or Google's privacy terms; we never receive it, and it is not used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Use of Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can turn it off in Settings at any time, or withdraw access in the Health app on iPhone or in Health Connect's app permissions on Android.

Exports you choose to share

You can export your session history, including any moods and notes, as a JSON file from Settings. An export happens only when you tap the button; you pick where the file goes using your phone's share sheet, and whatever app or service you send it to handles it under its own policy.

Settings › Restore from backup reads a backup file that you pick yourself with your phone's file picker and replaces the app's data on this device with it. The file is read only on your phone.

Notifications

The app uses local notifications in three ways: as a backup so your bells still ring if the phone suspends the app during a session and, if you turn them on, a daily reminder to sit and mindfulness bells through the day. All are scheduled on the device; no push server is involved. The permission request appears the first time it is needed, and you can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

Home-screen widget

The widget (on the Home Screen, and on iPhone also the Lock Screen) shows your streak, today's minutes, this week's minutes and your total number of sits. To draw it, the app saves that short summary in storage shared only with its own widget on your phone (an App Group on iPhone, the app's private files on Android). It never includes moods or notes and never leaves the device.

App updates

To receive bug fixes without a full App Store update, Calmwater checks Expo's update service (EAS Update, run by Expo, 650 Industries) when it starts. That request contains the app's version, your platform and an update channel name, and Expo's servers see your IP address as with any download. It carries none of the data you enter in the app and no identifier that links to you. Expo's handling is described in the Expo privacy policy.

Crash reports

If Calmwater crashes or runs into an error, it sends a crash report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), the service we use to find and fix bugs. A report holds the technical details of the problem: the error and where in the app's code it happened, the app version, and facts about the phone at that moment (model, operating system, language, free memory and storage), plus a random installation ID created by the crash reporter that is not linked to your name, email or any account. It does not include the entries, notes or other information you keep in the app; before a report leaves your phone, the app also removes user details, the device's name, log lines and the addresses of web requests. Nothing is sent while the app works normally: no usage tracking, no screenshots, no performance monitoring. Sentry does not store your IP address with these reports and deletes them automatically within 90 days. Sentry's handling is described in the Sentry privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Paid features are sold through the App Store and Google Play. To unlock them and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier (a random ID generated on your device, not linked to your name or email) and purchase receipt information from the store. It uses this to confirm which features you have paid for. RevenueCat's handling of that data is described in the RevenueCat privacy policy. Apple and Google process the payment itself under their own privacy policies; we never see your payment details.

On iPhone and iPad, the app also asks Apple for an anonymous attribution token and passes it to RevenueCat. If you installed the app from an ad in App Store search results (Apple Search Ads), this tells us which ad campaign it came from; otherwise it says nothing. The token contains no personal information and is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking in this version of the app.
  • No advertising or ad networks.
  • No accounts, sign-in or email collection.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data with anyone.

Children

Calmwater is a general-purpose app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; as described above, the app does not collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Because the data is stored only on your device, uninstalling Calmwater removes all of it. You can also delete individual records inside the app, or everything at once from Settings. If you exported a file, delete that file wherever you saved it. There is nothing for us to delete on our side, because we never received it. Crash reports are deleted automatically within 90 days. Apart from those, the one thing held off your device is the anonymous purchase record described under In-app purchases. To have it deleted, email support@foursailsapps.com from the device you bought on, name the app, and we will ask RevenueCat to erase the record for that device within 30 days; your store receipt with Google or Apple is unaffected.

Future changes

If a future version adds features that move data off the device, we will update this policy first and tell you inside the app before anything is sent. Features like that will be opt-in. The effective date at the top of this page changes whenever the policy does.
