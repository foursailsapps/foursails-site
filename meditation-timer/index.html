Calmwater

A quiet timer with real bells. Interval bells, presets and streaks, and nothing to read or listen to unless you want it.

Listed in the stores as "Calmwater: Meditation Timer". For iPhone and Android.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Set any length or pick a preset, with an optional preparation countdown before the first bell.
  • Interval bells every few minutes, or at the exact minutes you choose (Pro).
  • Five bell sounds made for the app: singing bowl, crystal bowl, temple bell, chime and gong.
  • "Just sit" mode: no end time, interval bells until you stop.
  • Bells keep ringing with the screen locked or the app in the background.
  • Streaks, totals, weekly bars and a 12-week calendar of your practice. Ten languages.

Free and Pro

Free includes the full timer (any length, preparation countdown, interval bells every few minutes, "just sit" mode), three bells, one saved preset, a mood and note after each sit, the daily reminder, the widget, Apple Health and history and stats for the last 7 days. Pro adds bells through the day, brown and pink noise, all five bells, bells at exact minutes or random moments, an end bell struck two or three times, unlimited presets and unlimited history with the full calendar. Pro is an annual subscription that starts with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase, billed by the App Store or Google Play.
