Relationship Counter

Count the days together, never miss an anniversary, and share it all with your partner's phone.

Listed in the stores as "Relationship Counter: Days". For iPhone and Android.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • The day count since you got together, with years, months and days.
  • Automatic milestones (100 days, 1,000 days and so on) and yearly anniversaries.
  • Your own dates: birthdays, first trip, first date, each with a reminder if you want one.
  • Optional sharing: pair with your partner's phone using a six-digit code and both phones stay in step.
  • A home-screen widget that counts up by itself every day.
  • Ten languages; reminders at the time you choose.

Free and Pro

Free includes the counter, automatic milestones, up to 3 of your own dates, partner sharing, the widget and reminders. Pro adds unlimited dates of your own, a reminder for every day-count milestone, and JSON export. Pro is a weekly subscription with a free trial, or an annual subscription, billed by the App Store or Google Play.

Privacy and support

Relationship Counter keeps your data on your device and has no ads, accounts or analytics. Sharing with a partner is optional and only starts when you pair the two phones. Read the Relationship Counter privacy policy or visit Relationship Counter support for answers to common questions. Email relationship-counter@foursailsapps.com any time.
