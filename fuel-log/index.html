Fuel Log

Every fill-up, logged. Real MPG, cost per mile and service reminders for all your vehicles.

Listed in the stores as "Fuel Log: Gas Mileage & Service". For iPhone and Android.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

Coming soon. Fuel Log is in final testing and store links will appear here when it is live.

What it does

  • Log a fill-up in ten seconds: date, odometer, volume and price, with partial tanks handled correctly.
  • See efficiency (MPG, L/100 km or km/L), cost per mile and monthly spend per vehicle.
  • Service reminders by mileage, by date, or both, with a notification on the due date and an in-app alert when a mileage interval is reached.
  • Multiple vehicles, each with its own history and reminders (Pro).
  • Export fill-ups and service history as CSV, or everything as JSON, straight from the share sheet.
  • Gas or electric vehicles (kWh per mile), ten languages, miles or kilometres, gallons or litres.
