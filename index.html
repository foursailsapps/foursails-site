How we work

  • Local first. Your data lives in a database on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to export or share it.
  • No ads, no tracking. Our apps have no advertising and no analytics in their current versions.
  • Fair pricing. Every app has a useful free tier and a clearly marked paid upgrade, handled by the App Store or Google Play.
  • Real support. Email support@foursailsapps.com and a person who works on the app will answer.
