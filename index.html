How we work

  • Local first. Your data lives on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to export it, or to share it with your partner in the two apps built for couples.
  • No ads, no tracking. Our apps have no advertising and no analytics in their current versions.
  • Fair pricing. Every app has a useful free tier and a clearly marked paid upgrade, handled by the App Store or Google Play. Apps you only need for a season, like a wedding or a move, are a one-time purchase.
  • Real support. Email support@foursailsapps.com and a person who works on the app will answer.
