Pregnancy Tools

A kick counter, a contraction timer and a feeding log in one calm app. Big buttons, no articles, no ads.

Listed in the stores as "Pregnancy Tools: Kicks & Feeds". For iPhone and Android.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Kick counter: one big button to tap for each movement, with time to ten and your average.
  • Contraction timer: tap when it starts and when it ends; see length, spacing and the last hour at a glance.
  • Feeding log: time left and right breast feeds, log bottles in ml or oz, and see which side is next.
  • History by day for all three, with CSV export for your midwife or doctor (Pro).
  • No notifications, no articles, no due-date content: just the three tools.
  • Ten languages.

Free and Pro

Free includes every tool with unlimited use and the last 7 days of kick sessions and feeds. Pro adds your full history and CSV exports of kick sessions, contractions and feeds. Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription), billed by the App Store or Google Play.
